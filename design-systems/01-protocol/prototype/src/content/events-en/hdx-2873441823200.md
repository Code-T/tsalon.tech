## About the Event

T Salon is back in Shanghai with TokenRank. This time we want a lighter, more developer-facing offline salon — "You've Got Ideas!"

Building a working AI demo is easy; the hard part is explaining it clearly and finding people willing to sharpen it with you. This event skips the long speaker lineup. The main thread is **show** and **talk**: whether you are building a side project, researching an interesting direction, or simply have thoughts on AI applications, come share your "idea" with people who are actually building.

We have reserved an offline space for about 20 seats and packed a complete three-hour loop — from check-in and opening to project showcase and open connecting. We hope you leave not with a slide deck, but with a few people worth following up with.
